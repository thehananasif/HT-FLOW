# HT Flow 0.1.0 preview 6

Sixth preview build of HT Flow, a Windows order-flow trading platform. Everything runs locally: accounts, settings and recorded market data stay on your PC.

**This is a preview.** Trading is paper only. The installer is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen shows "unknown publisher" the first time: choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## Downloads

- `HTFlow-v0.1.0-preview.6-windows-x64-setup.exe`: per-user installer (no admin rights needed).
- `HTFlow-v0.1.0-preview.6-windows-x64.msi`: MSI package of the same app, for managed installs.
- `HTFlow-v0.1.0-preview.6-windows-x64-portable.zip`: unzip anywhere and run `HT Flow.exe`. Data is stored next to the app.

## New in preview 6

**Fixes you asked for**

- **All coins on every exchange:** symbol lists no longer time out (gzip, longer timeouts), a failed exchange retries automatically and shows an error with **Retry**, and the picker shows every symbol (the 400-row cap is gone) with a count per exchange.
- **"Day Load" removed — all history loads automatically:** a chart opens in about 3 requests (was up to 60, ~25 s on busy Binance symbols), then older history fills in the background as far back as the exchange provides. A spinner replaces the blank chart.
- **Paper trading P&L:** open and realized P&L now update correctly (orders were matched to the wrong symbol id and the price stopped reaching the engine). P&L also shows in the status bar and DOM; trade history survives a restart.
- **Trading Panel layout:** no more clipped / sideways-scrolling panel; the drawing toolbar gets a **More tools (⋯)** menu on small screens; Settings and Strategies scroll so every control is reachable.
- **Every timeframe + custom:** ticks, seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks and calendar months, favourites as toolbar buttons, **Add custom interval…**, and quick typing on a chart (`15`, `4h`, `1D`, `3M`).
- **Replay:** Stop returns to live, replay from exchange history without a recording, no silent blank chart, no freeze at 1000×, P&L includes the open position — 10 fixes in total.
- **Real News, RSS and Economic Calendar:** free public feeds (crypto news, Federal Reserve, ECB, Bank of England, SEC, CFTC, EIA) and a public weekly economic calendar; add your own RSS feeds. Sample data only appears offline with no cache, clearly labelled.
- **Indicators:** 276 of 281 now work (Spread, Ratio, Correlation, Beta with a second symbol; Account Info; Position Sizing; Options Position / Risk Profile; Custom Formula editor; `.htind` scripts; 4 COT indicators). Applying 10 indicators on 50,000 bars no longer freezes the app (4.5 s → 0.2 s) and live ticks cost 1.5 ms instead of 316 ms.
- **Web Pages:** open in their own window in the desktop app (no access to app commands, unsafe links blocked), with bookmarks, allowed-site list and **Open in system browser**.
- **Split right / Split down / Detach:** fixed, and windows and workspaces can now open in **separate OS windows** (multi-monitor) that stay in sync.

**New features**

- **AI page:** bring your own API key for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, any OpenAI-compatible service or a local model (Ollama / LM Studio). The assistant can see the HT Flow window (asks first by default) and your chart context. **Agent mode** can trade with tools — paper account by default, hard limits, confirm-each-trade, audit log and a kill switch. Keys stay encrypted on your PC.
- **HT Editor:** write your own strategy code in the Strategies window (4 templates, autocomplete, error markers), then backtest, run on paper or deploy to your VPS. Code runs in a sandbox with no internet; every order passes your risk limits.
- **User Guide:** 17 articles with 82 screenshots (F1, Help menu, "?" on every panel), with search.
- **New branding:** new app icon, the HANAN TRADE logo on the login screen, splash and About dialog, and an animated footprint chart behind the login card.
- **Real COT data** from the CFTC (38 markets, weekly refresh, offline cache).
- **Faster chart drawing:** WebGL2 (graphics card) renderer, used automatically on a real GPU.
- **Log files and backups:** daily log files (secrets removed), automatic profile backups with **Back up now**, and the graphics-card setting now applies to the whole app (after a restart).

## What's in it

- **Accounts:** local accounts with encrypted settings and vault, 24-word recovery code, PIN, and export / import of `.htprofile` files.
- **Heatmap window:** standalone liquidity heatmap with gradients, bubbles, iceberg and spoof marks, drawing tools and a 3D mode.
- **Workspaces manager:** save, load, duplicate and export workspaces as `.htworkspace` files, 8 starter workspaces, Layout Settings, Clone window and linked windows.
- **Dashboard:** docking windows, layout presets, workspace tabs, link groups, Command Palette (Ctrl+K), Settings (Ctrl+,), a guided tour and a User Guide (F1).
- **Charts:** Candles + Volume Profile, Footprint (21 modes), Liquidity Map, Order Flow (Dark / Light), Imbalance, TPO, Multi-timeframe, Volume Histogram and Delta Profile.
  - Bar types: time, tick, volume, range variants, Renko, Point & Figure, Kagi, Line Break and Heikin-Ashi.
  - Chart Settings (Ctrl+S), templates, synced crosshair, log / % scales, Go to date and a data window.
- **Drawings and indicators:** 67+ drawing tools with alerts on cross, and 276 of 281 catalog indicators (presets included).
- **DOM and trading:** Smart DOM with 13 templates and a designer. The paper engine supports market, limit, stop, stop-limit, MIT, trailing and iceberg orders, ATM templates, drag-to-modify orders on the chart, and multiple paper accounts.
- **Order-flow tools:** Smart Tape, Bid/Ask Tape, All Prices, Market Pressure, Price Change, DOM Pressure and Delta Divergence scanners.
- **Replay:** market replay at 0.25×–1000×, with a replay account, ghost bars and bookmarks.
- **Records and risk:** Trading Journal with statistics, Trading Activity, alerts, and risk rules with auto-flatten and lock.
- **Strategies:** strategy backtester and optimiser, strategies on charts (paper), HT Editor for your own strategy code and a VPS runner.
- **Analysis:** Options Strategy Analyser, Watchlist, Forward Curve (simulated prices), real RSS, News, Economic Calendar and COT data, and an AI assistant.
- **Market data:**
  - Free crypto feeds with every listed coin (desktop app): Binance (futures, spot, COIN-M), Bybit, OKX, Deribit, Bitget, Kraken Futures, Bitfinex, Phemex and WhiteBIT.
  - Local tick recorder and Data manager.
  - Built-in simulator for futures symbols.

## Known limits

- Futures / stock feeds that need your own account or vendor SDK (Rithmic, CQG, dxFeed, IB, …) are not connected yet. Those markets run on the simulator.
- The crypto connectors have not been soak-tested against live exchanges yet.
- The VPS runner, HT Editor and AI agent trade on paper only; live order routing needs a broker connector.
- Not yet tested on Windows by us: separate windows on two monitors, the new installer images, the HT Editor sandbox in the desktop app, and the AI providers with real keys.
- English only.
